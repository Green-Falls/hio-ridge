## What they all do, measured

- **The first screen is a wedding, not a building.** Five of six open on people: a couple, a bridal party, guests on a lawn. The one that opens on the building (Garden Gate) puts the headline over it in white at 68px.
- **A serif carries the brand**, at 36 to 89px: Georgia, Marcellus, Garamond, Cammron, Radiant. Two add a script word as a flourish (King's Hill, Noble House). Body faces are plain sans (Gotham, Degular, Poppins, Questrial) or PT Serif.
- **White pages, one warm accent.** Garden Gate terracotta #D4967D pills; Hardy Farm sage #8BA287 button and rust headings; Bear Mountain black squared buttons on grey; Granite Ridge navy. Nobody uses pure near-black-and-green hairlines.
- **The nav sells.** "Pricing", "Availability Calendar", "Open Dates", "Inquire", "Wedding Pricing", "Book a Stay". Garden Gate and Granite Ridge list the year's open dates; Hardy Farm has an availability calendar. Three of six show a price or a package on the site; the rest gate it behind a pamphlet or an inquiry.
- **Proof.** Reviews, award badges (Bear Mountain shows four), "Real Maine Weddings" galleries, vendor logos (Hardy Farm shows a vendor row).
- **Lodging is the differentiator they lead with.** Hardy Farm sleeps 32, Garden Gate 10 to 14, Bear Mountain is an inn, King's Hill is an inn. "Weekend weddings" is the frame: Friday noon to Sunday noon.
- **Weight is not a concern to them.** 1.7MB to 88MB first views. Ours is under 400KB. Nobody will choose a venue for that, but it is why ours will load on a phone in the Lakes Region and theirs will not.

## Where the first Hio Ridge build sits

Weak. Against this set it reads as the least like a wedding venue:

1. **Hero:** an empty barn facade in flat daylight, where every competitor shows a couple or a lit, dressed space. We have no wedding photos, but we do have the barn lit with chandeliers at night and the interior set for a ceremony, and we led with neither.
2. **No mark.** The nav is two words of text. The venue has a logo (the heart wreath) and a name with a device in it (the ampersand) and the site used neither above the footer.
3. **Type too timid.** Caslon at 60px in a 20ch column reads like a report. Competitors run their serif at 68 to 89px across the frame.
4. **No warmth in the palette.** Near-black on white with green links. The logo is cream roses, blush, greenery, gold bells and a walnut sign; none of it reached the page.
5. **Nav does not sell.** No Pricing, no Open Dates, no Gallery. Theirs do.
6. **No proof and no "weekend".** Fair, since there are no reviews or weddings yet. But there is a grand opening with a jazz band, a Chamber ribbon cutting, the Bridgton News article, and a four-generation story. None of that is on the home page above the fold.

## Where Hio Ridge can win

What no competitor claims, and what the research confirms Susan can:

- **The only alcohol-free, Sunday barn wedding in the Lakes Region.** Every competitor sells the bar (Garden Gate has two bars and requires $5M of liquor liability). Hio Ridge is the venue for families who do not want a bar at the center of the day, for Adventist and other Saturday-Sabbath couples, and for couples whose budget cannot carry an insured bar. Nobody else says it.
- **Price.** Nearest set runs $8,000 to $12,900 for the day. Hio Ridge's price is `[CONFIRM]`, but the town record says "small-scale" and Susan's posts say "pricing brochure". If the starting price sits under $6,000, it belongs on the site and on The Knot; the Garrick SEO lesson stands: The Knot publishes prices and outranks the venues that hide them.
- **The view.** Pleasant Mountain from the ceremony field is a real view a photographer will use. Granite Ridge sells the White Mountains the same way.
- **The story.** "Four generations" and "rebuilt from its own boards" is true and specific. Hardy Farm's "c.1750" and Garden Gate's "c.1817" show heritage sells; ours has people in it.
- **Weekday events and classes.** Showers, retreats, the parent retreats. Competitors are wedding-only or inn-first.
- **Speed and honesty.** Under 400KB; no stock, no invented reviews.

## Rules for the redesign, drawn from this

1. Open on the lit barn or the dressed room, never the facade. Add a couple the first time Susan has a wedding photographed.
2. Build the mark from the name: Caslon's italic ampersand is the device. Put the heart wreath where it belongs, large, once.
3. Run the display face at competitor scale and warm the palette from the logo (walnut, moss, gold, linen), one accent with one job.
4. Nav: The Barn, Weddings, Pricing, Events, Our Story, Inquire. A Pricing page that shows what is included and, once confirmed, the starting price.
5. Say the three things nobody else can in the first two screens: alcohol-free Sunday weddings, the Pleasant Mountain field, four generations.
6. Proof row: the Chamber ribbon cutting, the Bridgton News article, the open house. Real, dated, linked.
7. No captions. No script fonts. No stock.
