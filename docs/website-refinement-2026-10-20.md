# Website refinement after the c7e191a review

The previous preview repeated a large paired italic headline, photo and arrow in nearly every section. This revision changes the page composition and removes that formula.

## Changes
- A navy opening combines the existing barn and reception photographs. The headline identifies the real setting without narrating an imagined wedding.
- Ceremony and reception photographs share one continuous layout. Getting ready is supporting content.
- Removed ornamental ovals, the detached sunset section, repeated italic headings and decorative arrows. The existing primary R&SVP identity remains.
- Rental details answer the questions couples compare. Pricing requests are labeled accurately; no price, rain plan or seating arrangement is invented.
- Removed the multistep email-draft form. Direct email, text and phone links are explicit until a genuine form service is available. No visitor information is collected or stored by this preview.
- Brand explorations remain separate. Canonical content JSON, build, src, admin and source photography are unchanged.
