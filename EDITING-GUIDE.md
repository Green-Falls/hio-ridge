# Editing the Hio Ridge website

For Susan and Richard. Everything you can change is in one place, the editor. Nothing you do there can break the site permanently: if a save has a problem, the site keeps showing the last good version and Mike gets a note.

## Signing in (one time)

1. Go to **437hioridge.events/admin/** `[CONFIRM: final address]`.
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and use the account Mike set up for you. Save the password in your phone's password manager so it is there next time.
3. You will see a list on the left: **Contact and settings**, **Events page and upcoming events**, **Weddings page**.

Changes go live about a minute after you click **Save**. Refresh the page on your phone to see them.

## Add an upcoming event (about 2 minutes)

1. Open **Events page and upcoming events**.
2. Under **Upcoming events**, click **Add event**.
3. Fill in **Date and time** written out ("Sunday, October 18, 1 to 4 pm"), the **Name**, and the **Details** (cost, what to bring, how to sign up). Add a **Link** if there is a Facebook event or Eventbrite page.
4. Click **Save**.

Tips: delete an event after it happens (open it and click the trash icon). When there are no events, the page says to follow you on Facebook, so an empty list is fine.

## Change the line at the top of every page (about 1 minute)

1. Open **Contact and settings**.
2. Under **Announcement line**, change the **Text**. Keep it short; the editor will not accept more than 70 characters, so it fits on one line on a phone.
3. Uncheck **Show it** to hide the line.
4. Click **Save**.

## Answer a new question on the Weddings page (about 2 minutes)

1. Open **Weddings page**.
2. Under **Questions couples ask**, click **Add question**, type the question and the answer.
3. Drag to reorder. Click **Save**.

## Change a phone number or email (about 1 minute)

Open **Contact and settings** and change **Phone, as shown** and **Email**. If the phone number itself changes, also change **Phone, for dialing** (that is the version phones tap to call: +1 and then the ten digits, no dots).

## Photos

The photos on the site are chosen and sized by Mike so the site stays fast. To add or swap one, text or email the original photo to Mike. Full size from your phone is perfect.

## Where inquiries go

When someone fills in the form on the Inquire page, an email arrives at `[CONFIRM: Susan's address]` with everything they typed. A copy is also kept in the site's dashboard, so nothing is lost if an email goes astray. The first time, look in your spam folder and mark the message **Not spam** so the next ones land in your inbox.

## If something looks wrong

1. Refresh the page. Changes take about a minute.
2. Check that **Show it** is ticked on the announcement, or that the event has a name and a date.
3. Text Mike. Nothing you do in the editor can break the site permanently.

## A few house rules the site keeps for you

- No dashes used as punctuation. Use a period or a comma; the editor will say so if one slips in.
- Headings do not take exclamation marks on the site. Exclamation marks are fine on Facebook.
- Anything marked `[CONFIRM: ...]` on the site is a fact Mike is waiting on from you. Tell him the answer and he will take the bracket away.
