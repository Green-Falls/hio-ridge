R&SVP.
Three directions.

R&SVP is the primary name. Hio Ridge is secondary. The original wreath has character. This round explores how to carry that character into a usable identity, alongside two distinct alternatives. Each has an original vector emblem, its own typography and a website application.

Our recommendation: The family wreath.

01 · The family wreath

Recommended direction

A new rose wreath drawn as a heart, paired with the owners’ initials. Deep mulberry anchors the name, with pale rose behind the stationery. This keeps the floral character of the original sign.

The references and the limits.

  • Pentagram / Chatsworth: developing an existing family emblem into a flexible identity. This informed the method for Route 01, not its artwork.
  • No Two / Daisy’s Place: a distinct signature across signage and print. This informed application testing; no lettering or illustration was copied.
  • William Allen Farm: a local benchmark for explaining the wedding experience and rental options. Its services are not Hio Ridge’s services.
  • Baskervville, Marcellus and Gloock: original font sources. All three are supplied under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are included in this file’s source.

The existing identity is the starting point. The wreath drawing here is a new interpretation, not a trace. The two other directions are deliberate alternatives. These are concept studies, not approved replacement logos or trademark clearance.

All venue facts and photographs come from the project’s research and current site. Pricing, rental access hours and rain arrangements remain owner questions. This review page does not change the live venue website.
