Words with room to breathe.

Libre Caslon Display gives the name and headlines a softer, more substantial presence. Upright headlines do most of the work; italics are occasional.

The details should be easy.

DM Sans handles navigation, planning information and forms. Body text starts at 17px. Buttons use familiar sentence case.

PhotographsUse the real farm. Show the head table, ceremony field and getting ready rooms at useful sizes. No generated venue scenes, stock couples or photo captions.
CompositionPhotography and text share a deliberate grid. Varied image sizes give the page rhythm. Clear white space replaces ornamental dividers and repeated cards.
Voice“We” and “our.” A moment she can picture, followed by the information she needs. No invented testimonials, prices or service promises.
Paths to inquiryTour and brochure links set the form’s purpose. Date and guest count stay optional. The production form must deliver and confirm receipt before launch.
Next production workExtend this system to Weddings, Spaces, Photos, Pricing, Our Story and Events. Retain existing factual content and owner questions. Confirm rain arrangements, rental access, starting price and inclusions before publishing.
