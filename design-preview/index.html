Sundays and weekdays
We host weddings from May through October. A Sunday wedding gives travelling guests the option of arriving on Saturday. Compare Sunday and weekday quotes from your vendors as you plan your budget.
Weddings without alcohol
We host weddings without alcohol. Guests of all ages are welcome, and there’s no alcohol bill to add to your budget.
A prep kitchen for your caterer
Your outside caterer can put the finishing touches on your meal in our prep kitchen. Confirm the caterer requirements with Susan before hiring anyone.
Parking and access
We have parking for 66 cars, plus three accessible spaces in the driveway. A ramp leads to the main floor. We also have an ADA bathroom and a paved path to the patio.
Seating and rain arrangements
We host up to 135 guests across the venue. Seating varies between the main floor, loft and patio. Susan can review the dining layout and indoor ceremony arrangements for your guest count before booking.
