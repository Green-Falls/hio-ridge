No date yet? A season or year is enough to start.

Room for your guest list +

We host up to 135 guests across the venue. Seating varies between the main floor, loft and patio. Ask Susan to walk you through the dining layout for your guest count.

A Sunday or weekday wedding +

We host weddings from May through October. A Sunday gives travelling guests the option of arriving on Saturday. Ask your vendors about Sunday and weekday pricing as you plan your budget.

Celebrating without alcohol +

We host weddings without alcohol. Guests of all ages are welcome, and there’s no alcohol bill to add to your budget.

Tables, chairs and catering +

Tables and chairs are included. We have a prep kitchen for outside caterers. Susan can confirm rental inclusions and caterer requirements before you book.

Parking and access +

We have parking for 66 cars, plus three accessible spaces. A ramp leads to the main floor. We also have an ADA bathroom and a paved path to the patio.

Planning for rain +

Discuss indoor capacity and the rain plan with Susan before you book.
