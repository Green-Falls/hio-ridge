What the copy needs to do.

Help couples picture this farm

“We made the ceremony benches from the barn’s old floorboards.”

The mountain view draws them in. Details from Susan’s own story make the setting specific. Each section adds something useful about the ceremony, reception or getting ready.

Removed: repeated instructions to sit down, gather and imagine.

Help them compare before they commit

“You can get the brochure before arranging a tour.”

Capacity, season, available days and the alcohol policy appear early. Confirmed inclusions sit beside the pricing request. Seating and rain arrangements remain clear questions for Susan.

Removed: unrestricted caterer choice and vague reassurance.

The references and the limits.

  • Pentagram / Chatsworth: developing an existing family emblem into a flexible identity. This informed the method for Route 01, not its artwork.
  • No Two / Daisy’s Place: a distinct signature across signage and print. This informed application testing; no lettering or illustration was copied.
  • William Allen Farm: a local benchmark for explaining the wedding experience and rental options. Its services are not Hio Ridge’s services.
  • Baskervville and Marcellus: original font sources. Both are supplied under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are included in this file’s source.

The existing identity is the starting point. The botanical frame is a new interpretation of its floral detail. The shared initials provide a second direction. These are concept studies, not approved replacement logos or trademark clearance.

All venue facts and photographs come from the project’s research and current site. Pricing, rental access hours and rain arrangements remain owner questions. This review page does not change the live venue website.
