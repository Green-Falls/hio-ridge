## 6. Facts worth noting

- **Capacity:**
  - 135 total (main page description, and the May 24 post).
  - Seating signs in the photos:
    - Downstairs Side 1, 12 seats.
    - Downstairs Side 2, 12 seats.
    - Upper Side 2, 18 seats.
    - Upstairs Dining, 20 seats.
    - Downstairs rounds, 44-46 seats (five 5-foot rounds and one 6-foot rectangle).
    - Patio dining, 9 tables and 72 seats.
    - See MANIFEST rows for 2026-07-27, 2026-07-30 and 2026-08-19.
- **Land:**
  - The page description says 22 acres. The May 24 post says a 23-acre farm.
  - The Aug 5 history post says the Burnhams bought a 50-acre farm in 1941.
- **Barn:**
  - Built in 1820 ("206-year-old"), with the original beams and the original barn boards lining the interior.
  - New barn boards were cured in the barn for a year, and 200+ year-old windows from the house were installed.
  - The floor was lowered 12 inches to meet code.
  - Full occupancy approval came in late July 2026.
  - The barn has a main floor and a loft/upstairs. The attached Ell holds the bridal and groom changing rooms.
- **Amenities:**
  - Bridal room with a dress hook, vanity, arched mirror and chandelier.
  - Groom room with a mirror.
  - Caterer prep kitchen with barn doors.
  - ADA bathroom and accessible ramp.
  - Patio with round tables and a deck overlooking the field.
  - Stone flower beds built from rocks found under the barn.
  - Boulder semi-circle seating in the field.
  - Ceremony benches made from the old barn floorboards; an arbor from barn beams was planned.
  - Tables and chairs included with rental, with 6-foot, 8-foot and 5-foot round tables.
  - Views of Pleasant Mountain.
  - Sources: Jul 19, Jul 27, Aug 3, Aug 6, Aug 14 and Sep 2 posts.
- **Parking:** A 66-vehicle lot plus 3 handicapped spaces in the driveway (May 24 post). Parking was "limited" for the Grand Opening, which required time slots.
- **Catering and alcohol:** The venue is alcohol-free. The May 24 post says "A family friendly venue with no alcohol," and the newer page uses the #alcoholfreevenue hashtag. A prep kitchen is provided for "your caterer," and outside caterers appear to be expected. Catering terms were not visible.
- **Days they book:**
  - The May 24 post says "Available - May to June, Sunday through Friday." That suggests no Saturdays, though the season wording may be a typo for May to October.
  - Sunday weddings are promoted (#sundaywedding).
  - They were "open all of October for events" in 2026 and are now taking 2027 bookings.
- **Faith / Seventh-day Adventist:**
  - The newer page's reel (Oct 13) uses #seventhdayadventist and #sdaweddings next to #sundaywedding and #alcoholfreevenue.
  - The Aug 21 post asks for jazz, country or gospel groups.
  - The Burnham history post mentions church service.
  - Taken together, the Sunday-through-Friday availability and the no-alcohol rule are consistent with a Saturday Sabbath. That is an inference, not a stated policy.
- **Pricing / packages:** No prices are posted. Facebook says to email for a "pricing brochure" (Sep 16 and Sep 20 posts). Class fees were $20 (Oct 1 class) and $5 (Oct 18 event).
- **How to book a tour:** Email, call or text Susan, or DM the page. Private tours are offered. Contact details used:
