Item #6         New Business

          6a.   RS&VP- Hio Ridge (Richard & Susan Voth Properties)
                437 Hio Ridge Rd
                Map 8 Lot 8
                       Event Barn

The Board discussed whether the application should be deemed complete. It was discussed that a site
plan (perimeter survey), stormwater drainage, financial capacity, traffic analysis, and sewer capacity
were all missing from the submission.

Rolf stated that he believes we should have a public hearing.

MOTION         Rolf Madsen moved that the application is sufficiently complete to initiate review
pending the above requirements are completed, second by Angie Cook
MOTION CARRIES 5/0

Susan stated that they would not like to come back before the Board until March 2025.

MOTION      Rolf Madsen moved to reconsider above motion, second by Cathy DiPietro
MOTION CARRIES 5/0

MOTION          Chair Brusini moved that the project is incomplete and requires septic, perimeter
survey, drainage, and financial capacity documentation to be declared complete, second by Rolf Madsen
MOTION CARRIES 5/0

Chair Brusini granted applicant, Susan Bobbitt-Voth an opportunity to explain the objective and
overview of the application.
Mrs. Bobbitt-Voth proceeded to give an overview of the application stating that her family has a long
history in Bridgton and expressed how meaningful this project is to her. She stated that she is
attempting to bring the community together and supply a space for small events such as craft nights and
business meetings.

Applicant to return in March 2025 with full submission.

Brian Murphy of 372 Hio Ridge Rd voiced concerns regarding traffic. He stated that several people walk
Hio Ridge Road on a regular basis and asked why a traffic study is not part of the Boards initial review.
The Board discussed and decided that the applicant is required to meet the standard, so this will not be
reconsidered.

MOTION          Rolf Madsen moved to provide letter that board has no objection to the moving of
