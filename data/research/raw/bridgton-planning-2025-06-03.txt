Item #5         Old Business

          5a.   R&SVP Event Barn
                Hio Ridge Rd
                Map 8 Lot 8
                        Public Hearing

Chair Brusini opened the Public Hearing.

Susan Bobbitt-Voth gave an overview of updates to the application since her last appearance before the
Board. She and her husband, Richard Voth, showed a PowerPoint presentation.

Abutters Alec Iby (179 Hio Ridge Rd), Matt Wood (380 Hio Ridge Rd), Brian and Susan Murphy (372 Hio
Ridge Rd), Kristen Murphy (Buswell Rd), and Denise Eldridge (341 Hio Ridge Rd) spoke in opposition of
the project. Concerns discussed included potential changes to the rural neighborhood atmosphere of
the area, traffic, noise, safety, and enforceability of conditions.

Richard Voth offered responses to the neighbors’ concerns. He stated that he and his wife will be
present for events and will be monitoring all these concerns.

Rolf Madsen discussed with the Board about the Boards ability to restrict alcohol. He also asked the
applicant for specific examples on how the noise limits will be enforced by the applicants and expressed
that he is concerned about the increase in traffic.
Evan Jones, representing the Voth’s, stated that the towns ordinance has limits that can be enforced.

Cathy DiPietro asked if the drafted contract has been passed by legal counsel yet. The Voth’s responded
that it has not been reviewed by legal counsel yet, but once they have finalized the contract, they will
clear it with an attorney.

Dan Harden asked Code Enforcement Officer, Brenda Day, if the Voth’s are allowed to have 2 driveway
cuts. Ms. Day stated that it is often discouraged but is allowed.

Chair Brusini asked the applicants why Sundays were selected as being a time for a wedding rather than
a Saturday. Mr. Voth explained that this is due to religious practices of himself and Mrs. Bobbitt-Voth.

MOTION      Chair Brusini moved to close the Public Hearing, second by Rolf Madsen
MOTION CARRIES 4/0

The Board began deliberations and set the following conditions:

    -   Amber colored lighting will be used in the outdoor section of the property

    -   Lights must be down-facing and not directed toward abutting lot lines on sides

    -   Applicant will construct a 3-sided fence around porta-potties

    -   Porta-potties to be removed from property within 7 days following an event

    -   Noise should be as indicated at the boundaries and the barn as indicated in the noise study

    -   Perimeter in aisle must be marked

    -   Downlighting on roadside

    -   No Parking signs on side of road at time of event

    -   No alcohol, cannabis or cigarette smoking allowed at time of events


MOTION         Dan Harden moved to tentatively approve R&SVP event barn as presented and
submitted with the conditions above, but withhold final judgement pending approval of Findings of Fact
and Conclusions of Law, second by Rolf Madsen
MOTION CARRIES 4/0
