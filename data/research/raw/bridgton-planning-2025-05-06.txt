          5a.   R&SVP Event Barn
                Hio Ridge Rd
                Map 8 Lot 8

Chair Brusini gave an overview of what has transpired thus far with this application. She stated that in
November 2024 the application was declared incomplete. At that time, the Board asked for further
information.

After reviewing the additional documentation submitted, the Board deemed the application complete.

MOTION      Chair Brusini moved to hold a Public Hearing for R&SVP Event Barn, second by Cathy
DiPietro
MOTION CARRIES 4/0

MOTION      Rolf Madsen moved to hold a Public Hearing on June 3, 2025, second by Dan Harden
MOTION CARRIES 4/0

Susan Bobbitt-Voth gave an overview of the changes since their last appearance before the Board. She
stated that this will be a small-scale operation as she and her husband’s primary residence is in
California. She stated that the barn will only be used in the months of May through October.
Evan Jones was contracted by Susan Bobbitt-Voth and Richard Voth to conduct a perimeter boundary
survey. He stated that the parking would be seasonal and on a grass lot, which will be delineated by
posts. Mr. Jones also stated that traffic numbers used in the report are accepted by DOT for wedding
venues with an average of 2.25 guests per vehicle. The event venue will hold up to 135 guests at a given
event, which is noted on the site plan. There will be a large parking lot behind the existing tree line
utilizing an existing entrance, and Voth’s will be adding 3 handicap spaces on the existing paved area.

Rolf Madsen asked where in the construction process the applicant is currently. Mr. Voth stated that the
electrical was inspected today by the state inspector, leaving stairs and drywall to be installed. Mr. Voth
also stated that they are hoping to have most of the construction completed by the end of Summer or
the beginning of Fall. The Voth’s also stated that their target opening date would be next year.

Chair Brusini asked the applicant to send pictures of interior and exterior progress to staff for the
record.

Rolf Madsen asked the applicant at the next meeting to go over the sound study and how the
information was gathered. Chair Brusini also asked for this and stated that it would be helpful also
provide an additional calculation.

Cathy DiPietro and Dan Harden also asked for clarification on the noise study at the next meeting. Dan
Harden expressed concern regarding what was used to monitor sound. He stated that through his own
personal experience, wedding DJ’s will have larger equipment with greater sound.

Chair Brusini asked for more information on whether there are wetlands on the property. Chair Brusini
also asked for more information on lighting.

Brian Murphy of 372 Hio Ridge Rd asked if the Board is requesting a new noise study. Mr. Murphy
expressed concern regarding the noise and asked the applicant to notify him so he could stand on his
lawn and hear the effects this could potentially have on him.

        5b.      Pleasant Mountain
                 Mountain Rd
                 Map 7 Lot 1
                        Observation Deck

Rolf Madsen recused himself.

Jim Seymour of Sebago Technics gave an overview of the proposed changes since last appearance
before the Board. He stated that all the requested information has been supplied.

Cathy DiPietro asked who would be filing the notice of intent for the DEP requirements. Mr. Seymour
stated that the contracted general contractor will notice, but no one has not been selected yet.

Chair Brusini asked for a floor plan and financial capacity letter to be submitted.

The Board began deliberations and set the following conditions:

        -     Water must meet conditions of public water supply
    -     Floor plan and dimensional information for bathroom area to be provided

    -     Financial capacity letter will be provided

    -     Restriction that deck is not to be used for outdoor events that include music or entertainment
          other than for what is needed for a wedding ceremony


MOTION          Dan Harden moved to tentatively approve the project as presented with the above
conditions but withhold final judgement pending approval of Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law,
second by Cathy DiPietro
MOTION CARRIES 3/0
